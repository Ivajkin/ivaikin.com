What we can discuss and how it starts

A few important answers.

What can I get in touch about?

Launching digital products and services that need software, IT infrastructure and coordinated teamwork: customer portals, internal systems, integrations and automation. Start by describing the situation and the result you need.

Can I invite you to an interview or event?

Yes. Send the topic, audience, format and proposed dates. Relevant themes include building digital products, leadership, delegation and organizing work.

What happens after my message?

We start by clarifying your situation and the result you need. If the project is a fit, we discuss the first stage, its scope and terms before work begins. For a partnership or invitation, we agree the purpose and format.
