Step 6 of 7 · Brief

A brief for email or Telegram

A few lines are enough. Leave anything you do not know yet open.

  1. Business and your role
  2. What is blocked, and why it matters now
  3. The result you need, and who it is for
  4. Current stage
  5. Target date, if any
  6. Budget range, if known
  7. Who decides and accepts the result
