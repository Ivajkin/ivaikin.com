# Hunt ladder funnel pages (ivaikin.com)

Separate pages for the funnel, next to the existing site. Existing pages, their text and design are not changed.
They sit under `/ladder/N/` (EN) and `/ru/ladder/N/` (RU), N = 1..7. They are `noindex`, not in `sitemap.xml`, not in `robots.txt`, not linked from any existing page.
Pages are generated by `scripts/build-funnel.mjs` (part of `npm run build`); copy comes only from text already on the homepages.

| Step | Hunt level | Page | Own stage event |
| :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- |
| 1 | unaware, entry | `/ladder/1/` | `ladder_1_landing` (first funnel page of the visit, any step) |
| 2 | problem-aware | `/ladder/2/` | `ladder_2_problem` (block seen 0.5 s) |
| 3 | solution-aware | `/ladder/3/` | `ladder_3_solution` |
| 4 | product-aware, proof | `/ladder/4/` | `ladder_4_proof` |
| 5 | most aware, offer | `/ladder/5/` | `ladder_5_offer` |
| 6 | intent | `/ladder/6/` | `ladder_6_intent` (page seen 0.5 s, `trigger=step_page`) |
| 7 | conversion, contact | `/ladder/7/` | `ladder_7_conversion` (direct contact click), `ladder_aux_contact_view` |

Every page links to the next step and to step 7 (link `data-ladder-contact`, counts as intent, `trigger=contact_step`).
Helper events on every page: `ladder_aux_scroll_50`, `ladder_aux_scroll_90`, `ladder_aux_time_30s`. Contact clicks also send `cta_email`, `cta_telegram`, `cta_bot`, `cta_calendar`, `cta_whatsapp`.

## Parameters (all events of these pages)

`entry = funnel_page` (existing homepages send `entry` only on `ladder_1_landing`, with the values `direct`, `search`, ...), `funnel_step` (1..7), `page_language` (`en`, `ru`). `ladder_1_landing` also sends `source` (coarse traffic category, same values as the homepage `entry`) and `landing` (path). `section`, `trigger`, `channel` as on the homepages.
Register `funnel_step` and `source` as event-scoped custom dimensions in GA4, next to `page_language`, `entry`, `trigger`, `channel`, `section`, `landing` (see `analytics-funnel-hunt.md`).

## A/B rule

- Metric: `ladder_7_conversion` rate per funnel-page visitor = users with `ladder_7_conversion` and `entry=funnel_page` / users with `ladder_1_landing` and `entry=funnel_page`. Comparison group: the same ratio for events with `entry` not equal to `funnel_page` (existing homepages), same period. A click is a proxy, not a confirmed lead.
- Minimum sample before any decision: 100 funnel-page visitors (`ladder_1_landing`). Review after 14 days.
- Success threshold: funnel-page rate at least equal to the existing-page rate in the same period.
- Rollback trigger: with at least 100 visitors the funnel-page rate is below half of the existing-page rate, or there are zero conversions, or a page error or a content problem is reported.
- Rollback: `git revert -m 1 <merge sha>` of the funnel-pages merge (removes the pages, `funnel.js`, `funnel.css`, the build step). Existing pages are not touched, so nothing else changes.

## Does tracking reach GA4 (checked 2026-11-18, live)

Loaded in a real browser against production: `gtag` is defined in the page head, and the collect requests to `google-analytics.com/g/collect` carry `ladder_1_landing`, `ladder_2_problem`, `ladder_4_proof`, `cta_telegram`, `contact_click`, `ladder_6_intent`, `ladder_7_conversion` on the homepages. Events are sent in batches (several `en=` in one request or in the body). If the GA4 Events report does not list `cta_*` / `ladder_*` yet: standard reports lag 24 to 48 hours for new event names, Realtime and DebugView show them at once, and event-scoped parameters are visible in reports only after the custom dimensions are created.
