Timothy Ivaikin attends Roirate's Anti-Crisis 2026 conference in Skolkovo

Entrepreneurs and product teams discussed how to validate demand, allocate budgets and make decisions under uncertainty.

Event date: 13 November 2026

Moscow. On 13 November 2026, entrepreneur, Edge Ecosystem founder and author Timothy Ivaikin attended "Anti-Crisis 2026: How to Stay Profitable in Unstable Times", a conference hosted by ROIRATE at the Stratos business centre in Skolkovo. Ivaikin also took part in the programme's hands-on systems simulation.

According to the organiser, the programme included six sessions: how people perceive a crisis, in-house media, launching a product with limited resources, opportunities of the Media Technology Cluster, product gamification, and a systems approach to strategy that connects people, systems and AI.

In unstable markets, a business owner's first question is where to put a limited budget. The answer starts with evidence: confirmed demand, repeat customers, clear ownership of the outcome and an agreed way to accept it. Faster work, including work done with AI, pays off when the organisation can review the extra output and turn it into a result for the customer.

Ivaikin works at the intersection of entrepreneurship, business models, processes and delivery. Edge Ecosystem builds CRM, internal portals, integrations and automation and provides turnaround management for digital projects. In this frame, a systems approach means agreeing on responsibilities, data and verification rules before choosing a tool.

Timothy Ivaikin is an entrepreneur, founder of Edge Ecosystem and author of Good Results and Shattering Limits. Programme: https://roirate.ru/product-ecology-workshop. Media and business contact: https://ivaikin.com/ · https://t.me/TIMOTHYIVAIKIN
