Timothy Ivaikin attends the Corporate Culture intensive in Moscow

The programme explored management decisions, accountability and team behaviour during organisational change.

Event date: 13 November 2026

Moscow. Entrepreneur, Edge Ecosystem founder and author Timothy Ivaikin attended “Corporate Culture, or the Art of Seeing People” on 13 November 2026. The organisers introduced him as a panel speaker. The programme combined a discussion of corporate culture with a Board of Directors simulation.

During preparation, Ivaikin identified two management questions: how to build an organisation in which new leaders can take responsibility for outcomes, and which principles help a growing team remain effective as new projects begin.

The workshop materials examined recurring management behaviours and the conditions in which they arise. A useful practical distinction is between observing a person's actions and assuming their motives. Before drawing conclusions, a manager can check whether the expected outcome is clear and whether the person has the information, authority and resources needed to deliver it.

This connects corporate culture to everyday decisions: how work is agreed, authority is delegated and results are accepted. Technology and automation can support those arrangements when their substance is clear.

Timothy Ivaikin is an entrepreneur, founder of Edge Ecosystem and author of Good Results and Shattering Limits. His work spans market needs, business models, processes and implementation.
