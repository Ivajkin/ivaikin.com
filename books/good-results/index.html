About this book

Good Results brings together practices for capturing unfinished matters, choosing useful actions and reviewing progress. It connects daily routines with personal values and a longer-term direction.

  • Goals and an inbox for unfinished matters
  • Choosing actions and finding causes
  • Daily routines, attention and weekly review
