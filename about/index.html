Working together

Start with the result people need.

  1. Understand the situation

    Who will use the product? What needs to change? We define the problem, the intended outcome and the boundaries of the work.

  2. Connect the parts

    Bring the software, infrastructure and people into one delivery plan, with clear responsibilities and dependencies.

  3. Put it into use

    Agree how the result will be accepted, deliver in bounded stages and plan for the people who will operate it.
